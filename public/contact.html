❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I receive after purchase? +

You receive a Steam account username and password that has the game already purchased. These are shared accounts, so you should play in Steam Offline Mode for uninterrupted access. Go to Steam → top left → Go Offline.

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery typically takes 15–30 minutes after we verify your transaction ID on WhatsApp. During peak hours it may take up to 1 hour. We're available 9 AM – 11 PM IST daily.

What does Lifetime Warranty mean? +

If the account credentials we provide ever stop working (password changed, account banned, etc.), we will send you a replacement account with the same game at absolutely no extra cost. This warranty lasts forever — not just for a limited period.

Can I play online / multiplayer? +

For online/multiplayer features, this may not work reliably since it's a shared account. These accounts are best suited for single-player games in offline mode. If you need multiplayer access, please contact us before purchasing.

Which payment methods do you accept? +

We accept: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), PayPal (Friends & Family), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Cryptocurrency (BTC, USDT, ETH, etc.), Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS), and Razorpay.

Is this safe and legal? +

We sell access to Steam accounts that have games purchased on them. This is a grey-area service. For personal use and offline play, it's generally safe. We have thousands of satisfied customers. Use at your own discretion. We do not condone violating Steam's ToS.
